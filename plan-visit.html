Plan Your Visit

A little preparation can save you time and make your visit easier. Follow these simple steps before leaving home.

How to Prepare

1

Choose Your Service

Identify the service you need and find a suitable service centre using QueueLess.

2

Check Opening Hours

Confirm the centre's opening hours before travelling to avoid arriving when it is closed.

3

Prepare Your Documents

Check which documents are required and carry the necessary identification and paperwork.

4

Check Requirements

Confirm any fees, forms or other requirements directly with the service provider.

5

Plan Your Journey

Check the centre's location, arrange transport and allow enough time for travelling and waiting.

6

Confirm Before Leaving

Contact the centre if you are unsure about its services, availability or current information.

Ready for Your Visit?

Prepare ahead, carry what you need and confirm important information before travelling.
