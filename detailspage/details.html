Service Details

Learn more about different services before planning your visit.

Health Centres

Health centres provide medical services, health advice, check-ups and treatment for different health conditions.

Opening Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

What to Check Before Visiting

  • Confirm the opening hours.
  • Check whether an appointment is required.
  • Confirm the available medical services.
  • Ask which documents you need to bring.

Government Offices

Government offices provide public services such as document applications, registration, licensing and other administrative services.

Opening Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

What to Check Before Visiting

  • Confirm the office's opening hours.
  • Check the documents required for your application.
  • Find out whether an appointment is needed.
  • Confirm any service fees.

Education Services

Education services help people access schools, colleges, universities, training programmes and educational support.

Opening Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM

What to Check Before Visiting

  • Confirm the institution's opening hours.
  • Check admission or registration requirements.
  • Ask which documents you need to bring.
  • Confirm application deadlines where applicable.

Digital Services

Digital services help people access online platforms, technology support, internet services and digital applications.

Opening Hours

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM

What to Check Before Visiting

  • Confirm whether physical attendance is required.
  • Check the services offered.
  • Carry your device if technical support is needed.
  • Ask which documents or account details are required.
